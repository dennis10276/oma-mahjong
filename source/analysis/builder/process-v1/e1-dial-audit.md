Definitions: a *puzzle* is the board content without clock.n. *Spare* s = clock.n - k with k = `engine.clockShortest` (peek pairs allowed, the game's rules). D of a point = mean of its 40-campaign blocks; SE from the capped geometric variance at D. A spare s in {1,2,3} *passes* when D(s) < 18.5, the next measured higher spare is monotone, D(s+1) <= D(s) + max(0.5, 2 SE of the difference) (synthesis section 3; when no higher spare is measured the tighter-side test D(s) <= D(s-1) + margin stands in; otherwise the tighter-side test D(s) <= D(s-1) + margin is NOT in the verdict and is counted separately as a two-sided column), and the tighter neighbour D(s-1) <= min(1.5 D(s), D(s)+3). If s-1 is not measured but a tighter spare is and already satisfies the limit, that counts as a pass (bound); otherwise the spare is *unknown*. Bands are by D at the spare being shipped.
