## Answers
**(a) What does real family Y look like where bot D is about 10?** Only 4 levels have D20 in 8-12 and a Y: 183 (Y 2.7), 190 (5.3), 192 (2.3), 222 (2.0, Dennis only). Mean Y 3.1 (Dennis 1.5). Of all 11 levels with D >= 8, Dennis won 10 in 1-2 tries. So today bot D 10 gives a real family Y of about 3 (range 2-5), not the 9 of the old 85-176 map. Level 196 (13.4 -> 12.7) is the one confirmation. Tiny sample, mostly Dennis.

**(b) Is "family about 10 tries at 250" on the bot scale consistent with 200-239?** Not supported. In 200-239 the bot reads mean D 5.0 (median 2.9), and Dennis needs 1.5 tries on average (first try on most levels, also on levels the bot rates 13-20). The only second view is Gerda at 200-209: 7.2 tries where the bot reads 2.6, so the slow family members are harder than the bot says, but they have no data at 210+. The measured family Y at 170-209 is 3-4 (13 three-player levels: mean 3.5), about a third of 10. A bot reading of 10 has so far meant real Y of about 3, so a curve calibrated on bot D alone (10 at 250) would, on this evidence, deliver about 3 to the family, with large spikes unpredicted. The data cannot show what Gerda and Mrs. D do at 210+.

**(c) How trustworthy is S for Dennis?** Weak. Spearman 0.15 (-0.04, 0.34). S is 1.0-2.5 on nearly all levels, Dennis needs 1-2. Dennis's 9 levels with >= 3 tries (176, 177, 182, 185, 196, 205, 217, 220, 230) have S 1.0-2.7 (not elevated); S's top levels (227: 12.4, 226: 6.3, 222: 4.7, 190: 4.3) were won in 1-2 tries (190: 2). S says "a skilled player wins first try", which Dennis mostly does, but it does not rank his difficult levels.
