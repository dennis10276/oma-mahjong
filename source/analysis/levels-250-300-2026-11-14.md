# Levels 250-300: hand-designed trap boards, and four display fixes (prepared 2026-11-14)

Prepared on branch `wip/release-levels-250-300` from `main` at 1.68. **Not released until Dennis says so.**

## What changes for players
- **Levels 250-300 are new boards** (51 levels). Each has a trap built from ice, locks, keys and the clock that are
  linked together; Dennis played and rated the kinds in ten local labs (12-14 November) and named where they go.
  15 chains with a known wrong idea, 15 older boards he gave 4 or 5 stars, 21 chains of four with a new wrong idea
  (waiting clock, lookalike key). No bot ratings: every board is judged by engine proofs and his verdicts.
- **Levels 301 and up** repeat 150-300 mirrored (the repeat span grows from 120 to 151 levels), so every level
  from 270 on also shows a different board than in 1.68.
- **A game saved on level 250 or higher starts again** after the update (`Hard.designVersion` gives these levels
  generation 9 through the table fields `designedFrom` / `designedVersion`). Levels below 250 are byte-identical
  and keep their saved games. Stars and progress are untouched. Grandma's track does not use this table.
- **Display fixes:** the clock's move counter stays inside the board (it was cut off on a clock in the top row or at
  the right edge); a frozen key or lock shows both its ice and its key/lock marker; tapping a frozen tile lights up
  every tile that can thaw it, also covered ones, with the same subtle glow as before; tapping a frozen or locked
  tile that cannot be taken shows what it needs (the thawing tiles, or the keys) instead of the tiles lying on it.

## Checks done on this branch
- Every new level: written with the game's own encoder, decoded back identical, stored solution wins under the
  live rules (`Hard.validSolution`), all 180 levels below 250 identical in packed and decoded form.
- Independent check of the boards (work branch, `tools/builder/bank/check_placed.js`): strict replay, clock number
  against the engine's proven shortest route, no face-down special tile, no visible twins together on the new chains.
- Tile size (Dennis 2026-11-14: big boards on the tens, tiles stay 54 px by moving other parts of the screen slightly): 96-tile
  boards sit only on 250, 260, 270, 280, 290 and 300. The pile gets 2 px of room on each side instead of 4, and a pile limited by
  the screen height takes about 17 px of padding from the top bar and the tray (`#game.compact`, `board.js fitGeo`); buttons and
  tray slots keep their size. On the 392 x 730 reference phone every 72-88 tile board of 250-300 is 54 px or more (nine-row
  boards 52.2 -> 54.1, seven-wide boards 53.9 -> 54.5). This fit applies to every level: tiles get about 1% bigger everywhere,
  up to 2 px on short screens. The screen shape used to choose and resume a board is still measured with the ordinary bars.
- Tests: `t_reorder_honest.js` and `t_clocks.js` pinned the 1.53 boards on 250-269 and the table length; they now pin
  everything below 250 and check the new generation. New: `t_ui_markers.js`, a clock-badge test in `t_hard.js`, `t_designed.js` (the real game wins five of the new
  levels by their stored route; an old save on 255 starts again; tile size and big boards on the tens for all 51).

## Not in this release
- Levels 301-341 of the work branch (heavier chains; six new wrong ideas are still being built).
- The level builder itself stays on `wip/cc-builder-loop` (`source/tools/builder/bank/`, list of the boards in
  `source/analysis/builder/bank/placed-all/placed.md`, Dennis's decisions in
  `source/analysis/builder/LEVEL-DESIGN-FACTS.md`); only the table it wrote is in the game.
- Level films (`www/pro`) exist up to level 249; films of 250+ appear as players win the new boards.
