## Dennis's forced-continuation observation

Dennis asks whether the bot recognizes that a position can remain possible only
through a specific future sequence. Existing continuation pruning can exhaust
known alternatives and preserve a winning prefix; it returns unresolved at an
unknown information boundary. It does not generally propagate future necessary
tray/key/clock ordering constraints backward through partially known positions.
Warming search is not a claim that this reasoning is implemented.

Preserve this as a strategy priority: identify necessary survival conditions and
keep their continuation feasible, including required tile order and reserved tray
space. Distinguish a proved forced route from a conditional plan through unknowns.
Timeouts and sampled agreement never prove uniqueness or supply observed memory.

claiming that capability. No new per-level exception or hidden-picture oracle.
