## Implemented public proof primitive, not yet a live policy

public_survival_continuation.js builds a bounded graph of known physical moves.
Actual new visibility, unknown transitions and unfinished states are POSSIBLE
frontiers. Backward propagation from all possible/winning frontiers identifies
closed known components that cannot reach either winning or new information.
Only those components and immediate physical losses reject a root.

If every other legal root is rejected, the remaining move is necessary IF the
position is solvable. The survivor may still be unresolved: this is not a claim
that it wins or that an unknown picture has a particular identity. Trace further
forced steps only while every alternative is rejected, stopping at new actual
information or a genuine branch. Missing root coverage prevents a global forced
claim. Returning to a partial root reopens its full legal action set. If backward
propagation times out, report no derived exclusions or forced path.
