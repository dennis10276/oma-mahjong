the older last-unrecovered-third-carrier preference, retain the actual accepted
state/action transitions of a physically failed attempt only when its starting
and ending picture/orientation/initial-role/known-key fingerprints are equal.

new tracked facts make it inactive. Position identities include alive tiles,

become hidden-picture deductions, death certificates or forbidden actions.
