## Optional implementation

retryFailedRouteLearning:true requires retryRiskLearning:true. In addition to
the older last-unrecovered-third-carrier preference, retain the actual accepted
state/action transitions of a physically failed attempt only when its starting
and ending picture/orientation/initial-role/known-key fingerprints are equal.
Unchanged tracked knowledge does not claim that no other learning occurred.
Availability proofs and other learned constraints remain outside this fingerprint.

Each route preference is conditional on that exact public knowledge fingerprint;
new tracked facts make it inactive. Position identities include alive tiles,
tray contents, open peek, relief, arrivals and remaining active-clock progress.
Multiple identical visits in one failed attempt count once. Penalties saturate3.
Censored attempts and clears teach no preferences; each controller owns its memory.
Entries explicitly say failedRoutePreference and provedLoss:false. They never
become hidden-picture deductions, death certificates or forbidden actions.

The existing bounded retry wrapper considers a lower-penalty alternative only
after the primary search and within its remaining node/deadline budget. It
preserves known wins and safe clock exits, and requires a known safe physical
prefix ending with tray<=2. Active-clock exploration remains disabled in the
initial candidate; enabling the older clock wrapper would still require a known
physical safe clock-exit prefix. Existing information recovery retains its budget.
