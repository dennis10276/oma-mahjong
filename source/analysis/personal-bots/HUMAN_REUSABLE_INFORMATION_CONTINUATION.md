## Optional continuation behavior

reusableContinueAfterInformation requires reusablePublicPlanning. With it, a
public-exposure or actual-peek witness is yielded to the caller while the search
iterator remains open. On a later same-root request it continues from that point
and can offer another frontier. A branch ends at its information boundary: the
planner never assigns the future picture or simulates having learned it.

Complete known wins still terminate and cache normally. Public prefixes remain
current-model validated; unknowns and exhaustion stay unresolved. Intermediate
leaf states join the visited set to avoid cycling back to identical frontiers.
If time expires during validation, retain the pending offer for a later current
deadline rather than silently losing it. Offering an alternative does not assert
that a previous unexecuted proposal physically failed; no invented failure fact
or guessed picture is stored. Role-information routes remain eligible.
