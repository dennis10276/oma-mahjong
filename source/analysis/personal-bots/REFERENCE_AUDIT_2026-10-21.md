  an unknown reveal. This does not prove their earlier policies optimal or
  the losses unavoidable. All six final dead regions were exhausted without
  numerical bounds. Safe detours that only postpone defeat are not rescues.

  Mrs. Doubtfire 109 but does not clear it. It remains optional: roughly twice

  triggers are retained with their unsuccessful results, not discarded.
- A global 1.5-pixel opacity inset fixes target-border antialiasing without
  widening symbol tolerances or consulting hidden identities. Very narrow
  edges can remain unknown. Public screenshot singleton observations, not

## Later human-possibility assessment

A replayable public-information winning trace establishes a human-legal route
on that deal under its stated timing, memory, aids and observation assumptions.
It does not establish that an ordinary person will find the route. Failed
bounded search means unresolved, never impossible. An impossibility claim
requires an independently checked exhaustive proof under explicit assumptions.
Actual practical human difficulty and personal prospective accuracy are
separate empirical questions.
